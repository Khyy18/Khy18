Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI personalize outreach? +

Our AI researches each prospect using multiple data sources, analyzes their company, role, recent activity, and pain points to craft highly personalized emails that feel hand-written.

Will this hurt my domain reputation? +

No. We include a built-in domain warmup system, enforce sending limits, handle SPF/DKIM/DMARC properly, and rotate across multiple domains to protect your reputation.

How long until I see results? +

Most clients see their first qualified meetings within 2-3 weeks. The system improves continuously as it learns which messages resonate with your target audience.

Can I review messages before they are sent? +

Yes. You can enable human-in-the-loop approval for any or all messages. Our auto-approve system handles routine messages while flagging edge cases for your review.

What integrations do you support? +

We integrate with Cal.com, Google Calendar, Slack, Telegram, and popular CRMs. Our API and webhook system allows custom integrations with any tool.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required. You can upgrade or cancel at any time.
